Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Homestead Manager ("we", "our", or "the app") is a personal farm and homestead management application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Homestead Manager collects only the data you directly enter into the app:

  • Animal records (rabbits, livestock, flocks) including names, breeds, dates, and health notes
  • Crop and field records
  • Inventory items and feed logs
  • Calendar events and reminders
  • Photos you choose to attach to animal profiles (stored locally on your device)

We do not collect your name, email address, location, or any personally identifiable information during normal use of the app.

2. Optional Cloud Backup (Farm Sync)

Homestead Manager includes an optional backup and sync feature. If you choose to enable it, a copy of your farm records is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and stored on Firebase Realtime Database (provided by Google LLC) under a randomly generated Backup Code or Farm Code. Anyone who has your code can access that copy, so keep it private and share a Farm Code only with people you want to sync with. This data is used solely to back up and sync your records between devices. We do not access, read, or share this data.

Firebase is subject to Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Photos and Camera

The app may request access to your camera and photo library if you choose to attach photos to animal profiles. Photos are stored only on your device and are never uploaded, including when backup/sync is turned on.

4. Notifications

Homestead Manager may send you local push notifications for events such as litter due dates, hatch dates, harvest reminders, and custom calendar events. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and no notification data is sent to external servers.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

Homestead Manager offers an auto-renewing subscription through the Apple App Store (on iPhone and iPad) and the Google Play Store (on Android). All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We do not collect or store any payment information, credit card numbers, or billing details. For information on how they handle payment data, see the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your data with any third parties. Your farm records belong to you. The only external service used is Firebase (for the optional backup feature, if enabled), as described above.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

All data entered into the app is stored locally on your device using an on-device database (SQLite). You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or using the "Wipe All Data" option in Settings. If you have used the optional cloud backup or farm sync, you can permanently delete the cloud copy at any time with "Delete cloud data" in the Sync section of Settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Homestead Manager is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
